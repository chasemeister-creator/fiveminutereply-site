The problem

If the answer comes the next morning, the customer has often booked someone else.

Someone's AC dies at dinner. They fill out your form or leave a voicemail. Nobody sees it until 8 am. By then they have called two other companies and one of them picked up.

That job walked because nobody replied. If it has happened to you, you know the feeling. Most owners know it and have not had time to fix it.

Questions

The ones owners ask before they buy

Do I have to get on a call?

No. Everything can happen by email. If you would rather talk, ask for a 10-minute call and we send a booking link. It is an option, never a requirement.

Do you need my passwords?

We never ask for your personal password. On platforms that support delegated users (Wix, Squarespace, HubSpot, Jobber, ServiceTitan, Google Business Profile) you add us with the minimum role. Where a platform has no delegated role, WordPress most often, you create a separate user for us and delete it at handoff. Either way the access is ours alone and you can revoke it in one click.

What if my website is locked by an agency or a builder?

If we cannot get edit access within three days of payment, we refund in full. If the site cannot be edited at all, we offer to build the fix as a new page on hosting you control, at a re-quoted price, or refund.

Will you text my customers?

Only with your written confirmation of the consent basis, and never in a sprint. Missed-call text-back and review requests need carrier registration and customer consent, so they come in month two of a retainer at the earliest.

What does "AI-assisted" actually mean?

The automations, copy, and code are produced with AI tools, then reviewed, tested, and delivered under Chasen Macer's direction. You get a human accountable for the result and the speed of the tools.

What happens if I'm not happy?

Sprints carry a 7-day no-questions refund. On any job, if something is off we over-deliver once, then offer a partial refund before we close it out. We do not dispute refund requests under $500.
